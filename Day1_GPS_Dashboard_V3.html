COACHING ANALYSIS

Strong wind day with competitive starts across the board. Johal delivered the team's best start at 14.5 kts on the center line. Marcos secured the pin end with 13.9 kts showing strong start line positioning. Team recovery was excellent: all three athletes showed 40-50% speed increase on leg 2 (avg 1.48x), indicating good boat handling and use of stronger downwind conditions. Key insight: the 11.3% gap to fleet top 5 on the start suggests India boys struggled with initial acceleration, but recovered well post-start.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXT RACE
1. Johal: Maintain center line aggressiveness - your 14.5 kts was fleet-competitive. Next race target 95%+ of top5.
2. Castro-Jurek: Focus on start acceleration drills - your 13.1 kts was 6+ knots behind your leg 2 speed. Work on weight distribution and sail trim in first 30 seconds.
3. Marcos: Excellent pin end execution (13.9 kts). Continue favoring port side starts when available - showed 10+ knot cushion vs center strategy.

COACHING ANALYSIS

Performance deteriorated in Race 2, with a critical concern: Castro-Jurek's start collapsed to 10.1 kts (-6.4 kts vs Race 1). All three athletes anchored port side, yet the overall fleet gap widened to -18.2%. Johal again led the team at 14.9 kts (94.3%), but Marcos and especially Castro-Jurek dragged the average down. Recovery factor improved to 1.54x, but this couldn't compensate for the poor line speed. The port bias suggests either the RC gave advantage to port or the team read the conditions incorrectly. Johal's consistency (14.5→14.9 kts) across races is noteworthy.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXT RACE
1. Castro-Jurek URGENT: 10.1 kts is unacceptable. Analyze video of start procedure - check boom height, crew weight, entry angle. You have 21 kts downwind speed; start should target 13-14 kts minimum.
2. Johal: Excellent consistency (94.3% of top5 in R2). Maintain Mid-Pin aggression - this is working. Target pin end again in R3.
3. Marcos: Solid 13.8 kts at pin, good VMG relative to position. Continue pin strategy but watch for over-trimming on approach.

COACHING ANALYSIS

Race 3 showed strategic rotation: Johal switched from center to pin (14.5 kts), while Castro-Jurek and Marcos took Mid-Pin positions. Fleet got faster (16.6 kts top5, +0.8 vs R2), but India's relative gap widened to -17.9%. Johal maintained speed consistency (R1: 14.5, R2: 14.9, R3: 14.5) but missed VMG optimization - his 8.02 VMG was poor given 14.5 kts speed. Recovery factor dropped to 1.42x (from 1.54x R2), suggesting harder conditions or rigging adjustments. Castro-Jurek improved to 12.6 kts from 10.1 kts, a +2.5 kt recovery from his Race 2 collapse.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXT RACE
1. Johal: Maintain pin end aggressiveness - 14.5 kts is your sweet spot. BUT: your VMG dropped to 8.02. Check angle to wind - you may be pointing too low. Target 9+ VMG next race (your R1 VMG was 9.54).
2. Castro-Jurek: Excellent recovery from 10.1 to 12.6 kts. Continue building on this momentum. Next goal: 13+ kts to close the gap.
3. Team: Recovery factor declining (1.48→1.54→1.42). Check boom vang and rig tension - may need adjustments as conditions evolve.
