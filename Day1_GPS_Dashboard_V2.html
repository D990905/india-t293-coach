COACHING RECOMMENDATION FOR NEXT RACE
  1. Set up 15-20m from the line, 10-12 seconds before the gun. This is the "acceleration runway" — enough distance to build speed, enough time to control arrival.
  2. Target 8+ kts approach speed (minimum). Practice close-reach approach angle, sheeting in progressively. At the gun, be at or above 9 kts.
  3. NEVER sit on the line waiting. 0m at 5 kts loses to 20m at 10 kts within 5 seconds of crossing.
  4. Practice "Time-on-Distance" drill: Approach from 50m, cross line at exactly 0:00 at max speed. Repeat 10x per session until approach speed is consistently 8+ kts.
  5. Consider Committee Boat side if wind is stable. Kiiver's 14+ kts approach from committee = 2nd position. Start with shorter Pin approaches first.
